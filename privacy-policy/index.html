3. Information We Collect

  • Account and business details: name, business name, email address, phone number, invoice details where applicable, and login credentials.
  • End-user / customer data entered by Clients: names, mobile phone numbers, and information needed to send reminders or notifications (for example due dates, appointment or payment details, stock or order information).
  • Messaging data: WhatsApp phone numbers, message templates, message content, delivery status, read status, timestamps, opt-in/opt-out records, and other message logs and metadata.
  • Website and enquiry data: details you submit through the Contact page or by email, phone or WhatsApp, and basic technical data such as browser type, device and IP address.
  • Usage and support data: activity logs, error logs and support correspondence.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as passwords of third-party services, financial card details or government identity numbers through our messaging features. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

4. How and Why We Use Information

  • To provide, operate and maintain our software products and services;
  • To deliver automated reminders, notifications and transactional messages requested by our Clients, including via the WhatsApp Business Platform;
  • To create and manage accounts, issue invoices where applicable and provide customer support;
  • To monitor message delivery, quality and abuse, and to keep our systems secure;
  • To respond to enquiries and to comply with legal obligations; and
  • To improve and troubleshoot our products.

We do not use end-user phone numbers or message content for advertising or for purposes unrelated to the service they were provided for.

5. WhatsApp Business Platform & Meta Compliance

  • Our WhatsApp messaging services operate in compliance with Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, WhatsApp Business Terms of Service and related Meta platform terms.
  • Opt-in consent: messages are sent only to users who have provided explicit opt-in consent to receive messages from the relevant business. Under our Terms & Conditions, Clients using our products must obtain valid opt-in consent before messaging any recipient.
  • Opt-out: users can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us at uttamboda25@gmail.com. Once a user opts out, we stop sending further messages to that number.
  • No sale or sharing of data: user data and phone numbers are never sold, rented or shared with third-party marketers, advertisers or data brokers.
  • Business Solution Providers: WhatsApp messages are routed through Meta-authorised Business Solution Providers (BSPs), such as Gupshup, under Meta's Business Terms. Message content and related metadata are shared with the BSP and Meta only as needed to deliver the message, and each is bound by its own privacy terms. They act as independent service providers for message transmission.

6. Sharing of Information

We share personal data only with:

  • Meta Platforms / WhatsApp and our Meta-authorised Business Solution Provider (such as Gupshup), solely to transmit WhatsApp messages;
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that store or process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • The relevant Client, for data belonging to that Client; and
  • Authorities or courts, where required by Indian law or a valid legal process.

Some of these providers, including Meta and its Business Solution Providers, may process data on servers located outside India.

7. Data Security

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to personal data is restricted to the proprietor and persons authorised by him, and is protected by login credentials.
  • Access credentials and API keys are kept confidential and are not shared with unauthorised persons.
  • We apply software and security updates and follow reasonable security practices appropriate to the data we handle.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a personal data breach we will notify affected parties and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules, you may:

  • Withdraw consent at any time, including opting out of messages by replying STOP; and

10. How to Request Access, Correction or Deletion of Your Data

Any user or Client can request access, correction or complete deletion of personal data by following these steps:

  1. Send an email to uttamboda25@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request - Access / Correction / Deletion".
  2. In the email, state your full name, the phone number and/or email address linked to your data, the product or service concerned (for example RemindBiz, Stock Management System, Farmers App or our website), and what you are requesting. For correction, include the corrected details.
  3. We may ask you to verify your identity (for example by replying from the registered email or confirming via the registered phone number) to protect your data from unauthorised requests.
  4. We will acknowledge your request within 3 working days and complete it within 30 days. For deletion, we will permanently delete your personal data from our live systems and confirm by email, except for records we are legally required to keep (such as invoices and accounting records), which will be kept only for the legally required period.

If your data was uploaded by a business using our software, you may also contact that business directly; we will assist the business in fulfilling your request. You can also request deletion by calling +91 63544 22522, but we will ask you to confirm the request by email.
