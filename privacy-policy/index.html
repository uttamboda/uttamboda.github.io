Last updated: 1 January 2026

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

UB Software Solutions, a Sole Proprietorship (Micro Enterprise - Software & IT Services) operated by Uttamkumar Rameshbhai Boda, Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-GJ-10-0089483, with its registered office at S/O Rameshbhai, Nathuvadla, Nathuvadala, Jamnagar, Gujarat - 361210, India ("UB Software Solutions", "we", "us"), is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and our software products, including RemindBiz, Stock Management System and Farmers App.

2. Information We Collect

  • Business and account information: business name, proprietor or contact person name, email address, phone number and login credentials.
  • Customer contact data: names and phone numbers of end customers that our business users upload or enter into our products in order to send reminders and notifications.
  • Message logs: message templates, delivery status, timestamps and related metadata for messages sent through our platform.
  • Billing information: invoice details, GSTIN (where applicable) and payment status. We do not store full card or bank credentials; payments are processed by third-party payment providers.
  • Website data: information you submit through our contact form, and basic technical data such as browser type and IP address in server logs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, secure and improve our software products and services.
  • To deliver reminders and notifications requested by our business users.
  • To issue invoices, manage subscriptions and provide customer support.
  • To comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

Where our business users upload data about their own customers, they act as the party deciding the purpose of messaging and we process that data on their behalf and on their instructions.

4. How We Protect Your Data

We apply reasonable security practices appropriate to the nature of the data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls limited to authorised personnel, logical separation of data between tenants, and regular review of access permissions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a data breach as required by law.

5. WhatsApp Business API & Third-Party Messaging Integrations

Our products send notifications using the official WhatsApp Business API through authorised Business Solution Providers. In doing so:

  • We comply with Meta's WhatsApp Business Policy and WhatsApp Business Terms of Service.
  • Opt-in: messages may be sent only to recipients who have given clear, affirmative opt-in consent to receive them from the sending business. Our business users are required to obtain and keep a record of this consent.
  • Opt-out: recipients can stop messages at any time by replying with a stop/opt-out keyword or by contacting the sending business or us at uttamboda25@gmail.com. Opt-out requests are honoured promptly and the number is excluded from further messaging.
  • No data selling: we do not sell, rent or trade personal data, phone numbers or message content to any third party, and we do not use WhatsApp data for advertising.
  • Message data is shared only with the messaging providers necessary to deliver the message (such as Meta/WhatsApp and our Business Solution Provider), and each is bound by its own privacy terms.

6. Sharing of Information

We share personal data only with (a) service providers that host our infrastructure, process payments or deliver messages, strictly to provide our services; (b) authorities where required by law; and (c) a successor in the event of a business transfer, subject to this policy. We do not sell personal data.

7. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Message logs are retained for up to 12 months for delivery, support and dispute purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Billing records are retained for the period required under applicable Indian tax and accounting laws. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To request deletion, email uttamboda25@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and details of the account or phone number concerned. We will verify the request and complete deletion within 30 days, except for records we are legally required to retain.

9. Cookies

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
